The Free plan includes the complete app (notes, diagrams, tasks, search, multiple vaults and themes) and the AI agent using your own key from one of the supported providers.

## 11. Sync, end-to-end encryption and password loss

Sync encrypts everything on your computer before sending it. The server stores packets it cannot open. The key to open them comes from your password, which we do not have.

For that reason, **there is no "forgot my password" that gives back the cloud copy**. If you lose your password, the synced copies become permanently unreadable, and neither we nor anyone else can recover them. We recommend keeping your password in a password manager.

What stays safe: the files on your computer, in Markdown, exactly as they were. You only lose the cloud copy and access to it from other computers.

A few practical points about sync:

- You turn it on, vault by vault. A vault you have not turned on is never sent.
- Files larger than 7 MiB are not synced. The app shows which ones were left out.
- If the same note is edited on two computers, the app does not merge the versions on its own: it shows a screen for you to choose.
- Each computer has its own trash.
- Sync only works while the app is open.
